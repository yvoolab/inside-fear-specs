TITLE
Inside Fear — step inside your own amygdala (Specs Lens · Learn AR with Specs)

DESCRIPTION
Someone snaps "How could you?!" and you freeze before you understand a word. Why? Inside Fear puts a brain in your room, lights up the amygdala, and lets it grow around you. Standing among the neurons, you follow your own fear response step by step: the sound reaching the thalamus, the fast shortcut into the amygdala, glutamate crossing a synapse next to you, the alarm to your heart and breathing behind you, the slow road that calms it down.

Made for secondary-school students learning the fear pathway, and for anyone who freezes when they are scolded.

Built for the Devpost "Learn AR with Specs" hackathon with Lens Studio 5.24 for Snap Spectacles (Specs). Recorded in the Lens Studio preview.

Flow shown: Start → "How could you?!" → amygdala lights up → Enter → 1 Sound arrives → 2 The fast shortcut → 3 Neurons fire → 4 Alarm goes out (behind you) → 5 The slow road checks → 6 Fear fades → Exit.

All voices are synthetic text-to-speech (Microsoft neural voices via edge-tts). No recordings of any person, no voice cloning, no music.

Planned with plan-lens, built with Claude Code + CLAD + Lens Studio MCP, tested with LEAF (5/5 criteria).
