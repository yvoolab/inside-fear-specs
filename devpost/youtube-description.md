TITLE
Inside Fear — Specs Lens demo (Learn AR with Specs hackathon)

DESCRIPTION
Step inside the brain's fear centre. A translucent brain floats in your room; a voice of blame lights up the amygdala; pinch Enter and it grows around you, turning the textbook fear pathway into a narrated scene of neurons, synapses and travelling signals.

Built for the Devpost "Learn AR with Specs" hackathon with Lens Studio 5.24 for Snap Spectacles (Specs). Recorded in the Lens Studio preview.

Flow shown: Start → amygdala lights up → Enter → 1 Stimulus → 2 Neurons fire → 3 Alarm goes out (behind you, follow the cue arrow) → 4 Fear fades → Exit.

All audio is synthetic: narration is offline neural text-to-speech (Piper); the blame voice and cues are generated tones. No recordings of any person, no voice cloning, no music.

Planned with plan-lens, built with Claude Code + CLAD + Lens Studio MCP, tested with LEAF (5/5 criteria).
